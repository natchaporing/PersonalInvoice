Banknote + document

Six ways to put the two ideas in one symbol: the banknote (guilloche, rosette, security thread) and the document (page, ruled lines, folded corner, seal). Names not applied.

1 · Folded note

A banknote with a dog-eared corner: money that is also a page. Reads instantly as both. Corner fold in amber.

2 · Sheet with a seal

A ruled document with a guilloche seal stamped over its corner, like an issued, signed tax invoice. The seal doubles as the PDF stamp.

3 · Split note / page

Half printed lines, half banknote rosette, joined by an amber security thread. The clearest “both halves” idea.

4 · Note on a page

A banknote laid on an invoice page: the invoice and the money it asks for. Most illustrative; the busiest at small sizes.

5 · Note-bordered page

A portrait page with a folded corner, banknote guilloche and a ฿ medallion. The most banknote-like; a single bold silhouette.

6 · Security thread

A clean white page with an amber security thread carrying the ฿ coin. The simplest and best at favicon size.
